What this is

Interactive view over the FPL-Core-Insights 2026/27 hierarchical Bayesian projection system — a component model (minutes → events → FPL scoring rules composition, never points regression) built and validated across the project's chat history. The embedded board is the depth-prior + minutes-shrinkage variant, the project's current best board as of the last synced session (Aug 4, 2026): two-season priors (older_weight=0.5), ownership-aware cold-start depth prior, evidence-weighted minutes shrinkage, team strength from reconstructed 25/26 Opta xG + ClubElo blending (live betting-odds feed swap not yet wired in — see Open Items).

Gameweek split — read this before trusting single-GW numbers

Derived, not modeled The synced project data contains each player's GW1–6 aggregate (mean, SD, full percentile posterior) but not a saved per-gameweek breakdown — that lives in run_2627.py's raw output, which wasn't part of what synced to this session. The per-GW numbers shown here are a fixture-weighted proportional split of the validated 6-GW total, computed as follows:

  • For each of a player's team's 6 fixtures, compute a defensive-ease signal (that GW's clean-sheet probability) and an attacking-ease signal (the opponent's expected goals conceded to this team that GW, i.e. the opponent's own xGA row).
  • Blend by position: GK/DEF = 75% defensive-ease + 25% attacking-ease; MID = 15% defensive-ease + 85% attacking-ease (MID scores the 1pt clean-sheet bonus too); FWD = 100% attacking-ease.
  • Normalize the blended weight across the player's 6 fixtures to average 1.0, then allocate mean_total/6 × share to each GW (shares sum to 6, so the 6 GW means always re-sum to the exact validated total). SD is allocated as sd_total × sqrt(share/6), consistent with the project's Gamma-Poisson/Beta-Binomial variance structure.

This is a reasonable allocation, not the model's true per-GW posterior — it doesn't capture e.g. rotation risk concentrated in cup weeks (tested null in this project, so low risk) or GW-specific lineup news. Treat single-GW columns as fixture-adjusted planning numbers; treat the GW1–6 total (and its full percentile spread, available by switching the GW selector back to "total") as the validated figure. Swap in the real per-GW file the moment run_2627.py's output is available and this caveat goes away.

Adaptability — encoding your own judgment

Two dials are built in, both persisted locally in your browser (no server, nothing lost on refresh):

  • Adj Δ column — a manual per-player point adjustment. Use it for anything not yet in the model: a confirmed XI change, a fee-floor judgment call, a regime-discount tweak, a differential punt theory you want to test against the board. It flows straight into "Adj Total" and can be sorted/exported like any other column.
  • Risk tilt slider — computes Score = mean + tilt×SD. Tilt toward +1 to surface high-ceiling/boom-bust picks (captaincy-tail framing, consistent with the project's validated "captaincy is a tail problem" finding); toward −1 to surface the safest floor picks. Sort by the Score column to use it.

Known model caveats (carried forward from the project)

  • Minutes/availability is the dominant, most-resolvable single-GW lever (~32% of variance) — a confirmed XI always overrides these priors.
  • No rotation multiplier — tested null (p=0.23), not built.
  • GK picked on team defence, not save volume — save volume is negatively correlated with points.
  • Ownership is the strongest predictor after minutes (Spearman ~0.48) — used as a start-probability prior input, not a projection shortcut.
  • Isak is the single most parameter-sensitive player in the board (EV range 16.9–28.5 across the older_weight sweep) — treat with wider-than-usual uncertainty until 2023-24 walk-forward data resolves it.
  • Team strength currently last-season Opta xG + ClubElo, not live 26/27 betting odds — the feed-swap machinery (betting_odds_ingest.py) exists but isn't fed live data yet. This is flagged in the project as the single biggest remaining accuracy gain.
  • 26/27 BPS rule changes are baked in (DEF −10.4%, MID +4.5%, FWD +4.1%, GK +1.9%), with a known refinement open: the DEF hit is bimodal (full-backs gain, centre-backs lose) and not yet split out.
